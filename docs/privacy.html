Privacy policy

Your course library

Ring Recall processes your selected map or camera scan on your device. The app stores saved course names, the map image, obstacle positions, visit order, entrance choices and practice results in its local library. It does not send your maps or results to the developer.

Permissions and tracking

The system picker gives Ring Recall access to the photos and files you choose. Camera permission lets you scan a paper map. You can change camera permission in Settings. Ring Recall does not require an account, show advertisements, track you across apps or use an analytics service.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases for the personal library pass. Ring Recall checks Apple's transaction and entitlement information to unlock or restore the pass. The developer does not receive the payment details you enter with Apple.

Keeping and deleting data

Saved courses and results stay in the app until you delete them or delete the app and its data. Delete a personal course from its menu to remove its saved image and associated practice results. Deleting the app removes the remaining local library, including practice-course history. Device backups may contain app data according to your backup settings. Manage those backups with the backup service you use.

This support website

GitHub Pages hosts the site and logs visitor IP addresses for security. The site contains no contact forms, analytics code or ad scripts. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
