Course help

Try a recall run

Open Clover Loop from Courses. Walk the numbered route, then choose Begin recall. Tap each obstacle in order. When the app asks for an entrance, tap the end where the dog enters. Finish the run to see missed visits and practice them again.

Add a personal map

Choose Add a course map. Select an image from Photos or an image or PDF from Files. On a device with a camera, you can scan a paper map. For a PDF, choose the page with your course. On a recognized Gamblers or Snooker sheet, tap the obstacles in your chosen running order. You can also review the printed numbers separately. Use a map you have permission to practice with.

Check numbers and entrances

Compare each visit with the printed map before saving. Under Check against original, open a flagged visit to see the source. Choose the next visit shown at the bottom to continue checking the map. If a printed number is in the wrong place, choose Correct printed number location and tap it on the original. Check which obstacle the visit uses and which end the dog enters. If Equipment type is flagged, choose the type shown on the map or confirm the current type. That choice applies to every visit to the same obstacle. If a number is missing, open Inspect unconfirmed numbers. When you find its printed label, tap its obstacle on the original or choose an earlier visit to the same equipment. You can also fill empty visits directly on the map. For hard-to-read maps, choose Search this map for more numbers. Compare the visit numbers. When new labels are available, choose Review new numbers on original to check them while keeping your assignments. You can also replace the whole diagram with the additional results. Undo restores the previous diagram.

Read a paper course

Photograph one full course in good light. If Ring Recall asks for another photo, follow the tip on the screen or choose a clearer file. If more than one course is visible, select the area for the course you want. When Ring Recall opens a visit on the original, check the printed number and equipment or entrance before you practice.

Manage maps and purchases

Three practice courses and one personal map are included. Open Course pass to unlock unlimited personal map storage with a one-time purchase. Choose Restore purchase on a device using the same Apple Account. This restores unlimited storage on that device. Each device maintains its own library.

Remove a course

Open the menu beside a personal map in Courses and choose Delete course. Confirm to delete the saved map and its practice history. The original file or photo in Files or Photos stays where you selected it.
